# 2026 Research Register

Research was refreshed against current public authority/standards sources in August 2026.

## High-confidence current findings

### ICAO
- USOAP CMA is continuous and risk-based, using current information to prioritize monitoring.
- The Safety Intelligence Manual (Doc 10159), 1st edition 2025, expands guidance for safety-data collection, processing, analysis, sharing and safety intelligence.
- GASP 2026–2028 sets targets around SSP implementation, safety-data systems and safety-data protection.

### EASA
- Air Operations Easy Access Rules Revision 24, March 2026, is available in PDF, online and machine-readable XML.
- EASA eRules marks future applicability dates and updates rule elements as amendments evolve.
- The current EU Ramp Inspection Programme is SAFA/SACA and uses 53 checklist items.
- EASA Standardisation is moving further toward continuous monitoring and maturity-informed oversight planning.

### FAA
- The Safety Assurance System is a standardized risk-based, data-supported oversight system used for certification, surveillance and Continued Operational Safety.

### Transport Canada
- Surveillance can be risk-based and can use assessments/inspections, audits, Process Inspections and Targeted Inspections.

### KCAA
- 29 revised regulations were gazetted/published in 2025 with transitional compliance requirements and enhanced surveillance; 9 more were pending publication in the official notice.
- KCAA air-operator surveillance guidance uses document/evidence review and inspection entry processes.
- KCAA has operational SPI submission/monitoring and publishes State-level safety indicators across operational domains.

### IATA
- Risk-Based IOSA uses airline-specific risk/context and maturity assessment rather than only uniform conformity auditing.
- ISAGO remains a distinct industry programme for ground handling providers and should not be conflated with State surveillance.

## Source discipline

The repository should never silently treat secondary summaries as authoritative. Each regulatory or inspection implementation should register its source authority, publication title, version/date, legal status, and retrieval date. Historical material should be explicitly versioned and labelled historical/reference.
