# Cost Intelligence

## Purpose
Model, calculate and analyze fees, charges and cost drivers across aviation certification, licensing, inspections and oversight services.

## Core model

```text
Jurisdiction
   ↓
Fee Schedule Version
   ↓
Fee Rule
   ↓
Service / Event
   ↓
Calculation
   ↓
Invoice
   ↓
Payment / Waiver / Refund
   ↓
Reconciliation
```

## Capabilities

- fee estimation
- effective-dated fee rules
- inspection cost estimation
- invoice validation
- payment reconciliation assistance
- waiver / exemption traceability
- cost-to-serve analytics
- workload-to-cost analysis
- revenue forecasting

## AI assistance

AI can explain why a charge was calculated, detect unusual invoices, forecast demand and help optimize resource deployment. It should never invent a fee or silently override an approved tariff.
