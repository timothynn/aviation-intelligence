# Organization Intelligence Domain

Organizations are modeled as aviation participants rather than simple customer records.

## Organization relationships

```text
Organization
 ├── Legal identity
 ├── Aviation roles
 │   ├── Operator
 │   ├── AOC holder
 │   ├── AMO
 │   ├── CAMO
 │   ├── ATO
 │   ├── Aerodrome operator
 │   └── ANSP
 ├── Bases / locations
 ├── Personnel
 ├── Aircraft
 ├── Certificates / approvals
 ├── Applications
 ├── Inspections / audits
 ├── Findings / corrective actions
 └── Safety / occurrence records
```

## AI capabilities

- organizational profile generation
- relationship discovery
- approval-impact analysis
- organizational risk signals
- duplicate/entity resolution
- change-impact analysis
- regulator briefing packs

All sensitive or authoritative relationships must be sourced from authoritative systems and retain provenance.
