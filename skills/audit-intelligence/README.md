# Audit Intelligence

Reusable support for aviation internal, authority, supplier and industry audit programmes.

## Audit lifecycle

```text
Annual / risk plan
      ↓
Scope & criteria
      ↓
Auditor assignment
      ↓
Pre-audit review
      ↓
On-site / remote audit
      ↓
Evidence sampling
      ↓
Conformance assessment
      ↓
Finding / observation
      ↓
Corrective action
      ↓
Verification
      ↓
Closure
```

## Supported patterns

- compliance audit
- process audit
- management-system audit
- supplier audit
- outsourced-service audit
- SMS audit
- quality audit
- industry audit
- authority standardisation audit
- USOAP-style State oversight assessment
- IOSA-style operational audit
- ISAGO-style ground-handling audit

## AI assistance

- risk-based audit scoping
- audit programme scheduling
- evidence sampling recommendations
- checklist guidance
- requirement/evidence matching
- finding drafting
- recurring weakness detection
- corrective-action analysis
- audit report drafting
- management-system maturity analysis

## Important distinction

An industry audit, regulator inspection and State oversight audit may share mechanics, but their authority, criteria, confidentiality and consequences differ. The scheme adapter must define those differences.
