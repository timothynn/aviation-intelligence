# State Safety Programme Intelligence

SSP intelligence connects State policy, risk management, safety assurance and promotion with operational evidence.

## Model

```text
Safety Policy / Objectives
        ↓
Hazard & Risk Register
        ↓
Safety Performance Indicators / Targets
        ↓
Oversight + Occurrence Data
        ↓
Safety Assurance
        ↓
Safety Intelligence
        ↓
Mitigation / Promotion
```

## AI capabilities

- occurrence classification
- hazard extraction
- risk clustering
- emerging-risk detection
- SPI trend analysis
- recurring oversight weakness detection
- safety briefing generation
- evidence-backed risk summaries

AI outputs are decision support. Safety-risk acceptance, regulatory action and SSP decisions remain with authorized personnel.
