# SSP Safety Intelligence

## Purpose
Support State Safety Programme governance, State safety risk management, safety assurance, safety promotion and safety intelligence.

## SSP pillars

1. State safety policy, objectives and resources
2. State safety risk management
3. State safety assurance
4. State safety promotion

## Data inputs

- occurrence reports
- inspection and audit findings
- certification / approval data
- accident / incident investigation data
- safety reports
- surveillance results
- SPI / SPT measurements
- hazard and risk registers
- industry intelligence

## AI capabilities

- occurrence classification
- hazard extraction
- risk clustering
- emerging-risk detection
- SPI forecasting
- trend and correlation analysis
- SSP gap assessment
- safety briefing generation
- source-backed safety Q&A

## Controls

Safety data classification, access control, protection requirements, provenance and human review are mandatory. Risk models must expose assumptions, data quality and uncertainty.
