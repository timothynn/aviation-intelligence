# Corpus licensing notes

The source registry is intentionally conservative. `repo_binary: false` means the repository should not mirror the source file without a separate redistribution-rights check.

The ingestion workflow is permitted to fetch documents from the official publisher for local/CI testing where access is public. Teams deploying the corpus commercially should review each publisher's terms and retain the original source URL, version and provenance metadata.

For EU legal texts, consult the applicable EUR-Lex reuse notice. EASA's Easy Access Rules pages also provide source/version metadata and may contain both legal documents and EASA-generated AMC/GM content; do not assume the entire consolidated PDF has identical licensing terms without verification.
